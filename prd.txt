# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Priti's Saaj Studio — Jewellery E-commerce Website

**Version:** 1.0  
**Project Type:** Mobile-first E-commerce Platform  
**Product Category:** Women's Jewellery

---

## 1. Product Overview

Priti's Saaj Studio साठी एक premium, mobile-friendly e-commerce website विकसित करणे, ज्याद्वारे customers विविध प्रकारचे jewellery products browse, purchase आणि track करू शकतील.

Website मध्ये customer shopping experience सोबत admin management system असेल, ज्यामुळे products, stock, orders, promotions आणि reports manage करता येतील.

## 2. Product Goals

- Brand ची professional online presence तयार करणे.
- Customers ना सहज jewellery products शोधता आणि खरेदी करता येणे.
- Mobile users साठी smooth आणि accessible shopping experience देणे.
- Product sizes आणि size charts स्पष्टपणे उपलब्ध करणे.
- Admin ला inventory आणि orders प्रभावीपणे manage करता येणे.
- Online payment आणि COD दोन्ही पर्याय उपलब्ध करणे.

## 3. Target Users

### Primary Users
महिलांचे jewellery products खरेदी करणारे online customers, विशेषतः mobile users.

### Secondary Users
Brand owner आणि authorized administrators, जे products, inventory, orders आणि promotions manage करतील.

## 4. User Roles

### Customer
- Login शिवाय products browse करणे.
- Registration आणि login.
- Profile आणि password update.
- Delivery addresses save आणि manage.
- Products size आणि quantity निवडणे.
- Cart आणि checkout.
- Online payment किंवा COD.
- Order history आणि delivery tracking.
- Purchased products वर ratings आणि reviews.

### Admin
- Dashboard आणि reports पाहणे.
- Products आणि categories manage करणे.
- Size-wise inventory manage करणे.
- Orders आणि delivery status update करणे.
- Homepage hero banners manage करणे.
- Coupons आणि discounts manage करणे.
- Customer reviews moderate करणे.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 Homepage
- Brand logo आणि navigation.
- Hero section with admin-managed promotional banner.
- Featured categories आणि bestsellers.
- Product highlights आणि promotional sections.
- Mobile-friendly navigation.

### 5.2 Product Catalogue
- Jewellery categories: Necklaces, Bangles आणि इतर accessories.
- Product listing आणि product detail pages.
- Search, category filters आणि sorting.
- Product images, descriptions आणि prices.
- Availability आणि size options.

### 5.3 Product Size Selection
- Size-dependent products साठी size selection आवश्यक.
- Bangles साठी size chart उपलब्ध.
- Size comparison आणि measurement guidance.
- Stock उपलब्ध नसलेले sizes निवडता येणार नाहीत.

### 5.4 Authentication and Profile
- Customer registration आणि login.
- Checkout साठी authentication आवश्यक.
- Profile information update.
- Password change.
- Saved addresses add, edit आणि delete.

### 5.5 Cart and Checkout
- Cart मध्ये products, sizes आणि quantities manage करणे.
- Product price आणि stock checkout वेळी पुन्हा validate करणे.
- Delivery address निवडणे किंवा नवीन address add करणे.
- Coupon apply करणे.
- Order summary आणि shipping charges दाखवणे.

### 5.6 Payments
- Online payment gateway.
- Cash on Delivery.
- Payment status आणि transaction details.
- Online payment failure handling.
- Duplicate order आणि payment prevention.

### 5.7 Orders and Delivery
- Customer order history.
- Order details आणि status tracking.
- Admin order processing आणि status updates.
- Order statuses: Pending, Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Cancelled.

### 5.8 Reviews and Ratings
- Verified purchasers product review देऊ शकतील.
- Rating आणि written feedback.
- Admin review moderation.
- Product rating summary.

### 5.9 Admin Management
- Product create, edit, archive आणि delete.
- Product images ImageKit द्वारे upload.
- Categories आणि size charts manage.
- Size-wise stock management.
- Hero banners manage.
- Coupons आणि discount rules manage.
- Orders आणि delivery status manage.
- Sales, orders आणि inventory reports.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

### Performance
- Mobile-first responsive design.
- Optimized product images.
- Fast page loading आणि lightweight transitions.
- Pagination किंवा lazy loading for product listings.

### Security
- Secure password hashing.
- Role-based access control.
- Server-side validation.
- Secure payment verification.
- Customer data protection.

### Usability
- Simple navigation आणि clear calls to action.
- Touch-friendly controls.
- Consistent brand styling.
- Accessible text contrast आणि form labels.

### Reliability
- Consistent order आणि stock management.
- Error handling आणि logging.
- Database backups.

## 7. MVP Scope

### Included
- Homepage आणि product catalogue.
- Customer authentication आणि profile.
- Product size selection आणि size chart.
- Cart आणि checkout.
- Online payment आणि COD.
- Order tracking.
- Admin product, stock, order, banner आणि coupon management.
- Reviews आणि basic reports.

### Future Enhancements
- Wishlist.
- WhatsApp notifications.
- Advanced analytics.
- Multiple languages.
- Shipping provider integration.

## 8. Success Criteria

- Customers login शिवाय products browse करू शकतात.
- Checkout पूर्वी login/register आवश्यक आहे.
- Size आणि stock योग्यरीत्या validate होतात.
- Online payment आणि COD orders योग्यरीत्या process होतात.
- Customers saved addresses आणि order tracking वापरू शकतात.
- Admin products, banners, coupons आणि inventory manage करू शकतो.
- Website mobile devices वर व्यवस्थित काम करते.

## 9. Assumptions and Dependencies

- Final product images, descriptions, prices आणि size charts client कडून मिळतील.
- ImageKit image storage आणि delivery साठी वापरले जाईल.
- Payment gateway आणि hosting provider budget नुसार निवडले जातील.
- Shipping charges आणि delivery policies client कडून निश्चित केल्या जातील.
- Demo मध्ये mock products आणि sample data वापरले जाऊ शकतात.
