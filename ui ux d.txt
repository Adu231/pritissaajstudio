# PRITI'S SAAJ STUDIO
## UI/UX DESIGN DOCUMENT

**Project:** Jewellery E-commerce Website  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Design Direction:** Sage Gold — Premium, Elegant & Mobile-First

---

## 1. Document Overview

This document defines the visual design system, page layouts, user interactions, and responsive design requirements for Priti's Saaj Studio.

The website will provide a premium online shopping experience for women's jewellery, with a strong focus on mobile usability, elegant product presentation, and a simple checkout process.

## 2. Design Goals

- Create a premium and elegant jewellery brand experience.
- Prioritize mobile users with a mobile-first responsive layout.
- Make product discovery and purchasing simple and intuitive.
- Present product images, prices, sizes, and availability clearly.
- Provide a smooth checkout experience with minimal friction.
- Maintain a consistent visual identity across customer and admin interfaces.
- Use lightweight transitions and loading effects without excessive animations.

## 3. Brand Identity & Visual Design

### 3.1 Design Theme

**Theme Name:** Sage Gold

The design will combine emerald green, sage, ivory, and gold to reflect the brand's jewellery identity.

The overall appearance should be elegant, luxurious, clean, and modern without feeling visually crowded.

### 3.2 Color Palette

| Color | Hex Code | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Emerald Green | #0B3D2E | Primary buttons, navigation, headings, selected states |
| Sage Green | #A7C4A0 | Secondary backgrounds, chips, soft accents |
| Gold | #D4AF37 | Highlights, borders, badges, decorative accents |
| Ivory | #F8F7F1 | Main page background |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cards, forms, modals |
| Charcoal | #1F1F1F | Body text and labels |

Color usage guidelines:

- Emerald green should be the primary brand color.
- Gold should be used sparingly for premium accents.
- Ivory should provide a warm and elegant background.
- Text contrast must remain readable, especially on mobile devices.
- Avoid excessive gradients and decorative effects.

| H2 | 24–30 px | 22–26 px |

## 6. Customer Interface Requirements

### 6.1 Homepage

The homepage should include:

- Header with brand logo, search, account, and cart icons.
- Responsive navigation for product categories.
- Admin-managed hero banner with image, headline, and CTA.
- Shop-by-category section.
- Featured products and bestsellers.
- Promotional or coupon section.
- Brand introduction section.
- Footer with contact details, policies, and social links.

The hero section should use premium jewellery imagery with clear text and a prominent shopping CTA.

### 6.2 Product Catalogue

The product catalogue should include:

- Responsive product grid.
- Product image, name, price, discount, and rating.
- Search by product name.
- Category filters.
- Price and availability filters.
- Size filters where applicable.
- Sorting by newest, price low-to-high, and price high-to-low.
- Pagination or lazy loading for large catalogues.

Mobile layout:
- Two product cards per row on mobile.
- Larger grids on tablet and desktop.
- Touch-friendly filter and sort controls.

### 6.3 Product Details Page

The product details page should include:

- Product image gallery with swipe support on mobile.
- Product name and description.
- Price and discount information.
- Availability and stock status.
- Size selection for size-dependent products.
- Size Guide link.
- Quantity selector.
- Add to Cart button.
- Buy Now button.
- Rating and customer reviews.
- Related products.

Size selection must be completed before adding a size-dependent product to the cart.

### 6.4 Size Guide

The size guide should be accessible from the product details page.

For bangles, it should include:

- Available sizes.
- Inner diameter.
- Inner circumference.
- Measurement instructions.
- Size comparison chart.

The final measurements and size labels must be confirmed by the client.

The size guide should open in a mobile-friendly modal or dedicated page.

### 6.5 Cart

The cart should display:

- Product image and name.
- Selected size.
- Quantity controls.
- Unit price.
- Remove item action.
- Subtotal.
- Coupon discount.
- Shipping charges.
- Final total.

The cart should clearly indicate when a product or selected size is unavailable.

### 6.6 Checkout

Checkout requires customer authentication.

Checkout flow:

1. Login or registration.
2. Select saved address or add a new address.
3. Select payment method.
4. Review order summary.
5. Place order.
6. Display order confirmation.

Payment options:

- Online payment.
- Cash on Delivery (COD).

The checkout interface should provide clear validation, loading, success, and failure states.

### 6.7 Customer Profile

The customer profile should include:

- View and edit personal information.
- Update password.
- Add, edit, and delete addresses.
- Set a default delivery address.
- View order history.
- View order details and delivery status.

### 6.8 Order Tracking

The order tracking page should display a visual timeline:

- Order Placed
- Confirmed
- Processing
- Shipped
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered

Cancelled orders should display a clear cancellation status.

Tracking numbers and courier details should be displayed when available.

### 6.9 Reviews and Ratings

Customers should be able to:

- Rate purchased products.
- Submit written reviews.
- View approved reviews.
- See aggregate product ratings.

Only verified purchasers should be allowed to submit reviews.

## 7. Admin Interface Requirements

### 7.1 Dashboard

The dashboard should display:

- Total sales.
- Total orders.
- Revenue summary.
- Low-stock products.
- Recent orders.
- Sales trends where data is available.

Provide quick links to product creation, inventory, and order management.

### 7.2 Product Management

Admin should be able to:

- Add, edit, and archive products.
- Manage product names, descriptions, categories, and prices.
- Upload and manage product images through ImageKit.
- Add multiple images per product.
- Configure product sizes and size charts.
- Manage size-wise stock.
- Set discounts and product availability.

### 7.3 Inventory Management

The inventory interface should include:

- Product-wise stock.
- Size-wise stock quantities.
- Low-stock indicators.
- Out-of-stock indicators.
- Stock update controls.

### 7.4 Order Management

Admin should be able to:

- View all orders.
- Search and filter orders.
- View customer and delivery information.
- View payment status.
- Update order status.
- View order details and selected product sizes.

### 7.5 Hero Banner Management

Admin should be able to:

- Upload banner images through ImageKit.
- Update banner title and subtitle.
- Configure CTA text and destination.
- Activate or deactivate banners.
- Set banner display order.

### 7.6 Coupon Management

Admin should be able to:

- Create and edit coupon codes.
- Configure percentage or fixed discounts.
- Set minimum order value.
- Set validity dates.
- Set usage limits.
- Activate or deactivate coupons.

### 7.7 Reviews Management

Admin should be able to:

- View submitted reviews.
- Approve or reject reviews.
- Hide inappropriate content.

### 7.8 Reports

The reports interface should provide:

- Sales reports.
- Order reports.
- Top-selling products.
- Inventory reports.
- Date-based filters.
- Export functionality if included in the project scope.

## 8. Interaction & Motion Design

The website should use subtle and lightweight interactions.

### Transitions

- Button hover and pressed states.
- Smooth navigation transitions.
- Modal open and close transitions.
- Subtle product card hover effects on desktop.
- Smooth cart updates.

Recommended duration: 150–250 ms.

### Loading States

- Skeleton loaders for product cards and page content.
- Lightweight gemstone-inspired loading indicator.
- Clear loading feedback during checkout and payment.
- Avoid long blocking animations.

### Feedback

- Success and error toast messages.
- Inline form validation.
- Clear disabled button states.
- Confirmation dialogs for destructive admin actions.

Avoid heavy parallax, autoplay video, and excessive animations.

## 9. Responsive Design Guidelines

The website must follow a mobile-first approach.

### Breakpoints

| Device | Width |
|---|---|
| Small mobile | Up to 480 px |
| Tablet | 481–768 px |
| Desktop | 769 px and above |

### Mobile Requirements

- Two-column product grid.
- Touch-friendly controls.
- Simple navigation.
- Readable typography.
- Responsive product image galleries.
- Mobile-friendly size charts.
- Easy-to-use checkout forms.
- No horizontal overflow.

All important customer journeys must work smoothly on common Android and iOS screen sizes.

## 11. Accessibility & Usability

- Use semantic HTML elements.
- Provide accessible labels for form fields.
- Maintain sufficient text contrast.
- Provide keyboard focus states.
- Use descriptive alt text for product images.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate status.
- Maintain practical touch target sizes of approximately 44 × 44 px.
- Keep navigation and checkout steps clear and consistent.

## 12. Design Acceptance Criteria

The design will be considered complete when:

- Customer pages are responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Customers can browse products without logging in.
- Authentication is required before checkout.
- Size selection and size guides work for applicable products.
- Cart and checkout clearly display selected sizes and order totals.
- Customer profile and saved addresses are accessible.
- Order history and tracking are easy to understand.
- Admin can manage products, images, inventory, banners, coupons, orders, and reviews.
- The approved Sage Gold theme and typography are consistently applied.
- Loading, empty, error, and success states are designed.
- Animations remain subtle and do not interfere with usability.

## 13. Assumptions & Dependencies

- Client will provide final product images, descriptions, prices, and categories.
- Client will confirm product size charts and measurements.
- ImageKit will be used for product image storage and delivery.
- Payment gateway and shipping provider will be finalized according to budget.
- Client will provide shipping, cancellation, return, and contact policies.
- Final logo and brand assets will be provided in suitable formats.
